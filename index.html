👋 About Me

👨‍💻 I am a Research Scientist at Google Research, where I lead the development of autonomous agents for long-horizon optimization in evolving environments, deployed to serve users in production. Earlier, I worked on post-training Gemini for specialized coding and reasoning (reaching #1 on the BIRD Text-to-SQL leaderboard).

🧬 Previously, as an Eric and Wendy Schmidt Center Postdoctoral Fellow at the Broad Institute of MIT and Harvard (with Caroline Uhler), I led the pretraining of a biomedical foundation model from scratch, from data pipelines to distributed training on GPU clusters, and applied it to discovery of new therapeutic targets in biomedicine.

☁️ During my PhD, I worked as a part-time research intern at Amazon Web Services for three years, designing linear-complexity attention for 1M-token contexts and developing scalable Transformer architectures (NodeFormer, DIFFormer and SGFormer) as widely used open-source models, with 1,300+ citations and 900+ GitHub stars in total. Earlier, I was an applied research intern at Tencent WeChat, building deep learning models for industry-scale recommender systems.

🎓 My research has been recognized with the Microsoft Research PhD Fellowship (11 recipients in Asia), the Baidu PhD Fellowship (10 recipients worldwide) and the Elsevier/Stanford World's Top 2% Scientists list in Artificial Intelligence. I received my Ph.D., M.E. and B.E. degrees from Shanghai Jiao Tong University, advised by Junchi Yan, and collaborated with David Wipf, Philip S. Yu, Hongyuan Zha and Michael Bronstein during my PhD. I serve as an Area Chair for NeurIPS and ICLR.
