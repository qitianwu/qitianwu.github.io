## Hi, I'm Qitian Wu 👋

🔬 I am a Research Scientist at **Google Research**, working on **LLM post-training and agents**. My recent projects:

- 🏆 **Gemini post-training for Text-to-SQL generation**: post-trained Gemini to the top of the [BIRD](https://bird-bench.github.io/) Text-to-SQL leaderboard.
  <br>*(Synthetic data generation, sandbox environments with tool use, reward models)*
- 🤖 **Agents for systems optimization**: built a closed-loop agent system that optimizes production code from evolving environments, deployed in compiler systems.
  <br>*(Agentic harness, long-horizon reasoning, endpoint serving)*
- 🌐 **Gemini reasoning for network-state observability**: partnered with infrastructure teams to scope large-scale cluster telemetry into an LLM reasoning task.
  <br>*(Data curation and filtering, evaluation pipelines)*

🧬 Previously, as an Eric and Wendy Schmidt Center Postdoctoral Fellow at the **Broad Institute of MIT and Harvard** (with Caroline Uhler),
I led the **pretraining of a multi-modal foundation model from scratch**, from data pipelines to distributed training on GPU clusters,
and applied it to discovery of therapeutic targets in biomedicine
([paper](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.07.31.741265v1)).

⚡ During my PhD, I worked as a part-time research intern at **Amazon Web Services** for three years, designing **linear-complexity attention**
that scales Transformers to 1M-token contexts on a single 16GB GPU, and releasing the resulting models as widely used open-source projects:
[NodeFormer](https://github.com/qitianwu/NodeFormer),
[SGFormer](https://github.com/qitianwu/SGFormer) and
[DIFFormer](https://github.com/qitianwu/DIFFormer),
with **1,300+ citations** and **⭐ 900+ GitHub stars** in total.
Earlier, I was an applied research intern at **Tencent WeChat**, building deep learning models for industry-scale recommender systems.

🎓 I received my Ph.D., M.E. and B.E. degrees from **Shanghai Jiao Tong University**, with my PhD advised by Junchi Yan.
My research has received **4,700+ citations** and been recognized with
the **Microsoft Research PhD Fellowship** (**11 recipients** in Asia), the **Baidu PhD Fellowship** (**10 recipients** worldwide)
and the Elsevier/Stanford **World's Top 2% Scientists** list in Artificial Intelligence.
I serve as an Area Chair for NeurIPS and ICLR.
